The Problem

Coaching quality is rising, but practical movement insight is still hard to access

Many sports organizations know technique matters, but they still rely on observation, fragmented tools, or systems that are too expensive for regular team use.

1
Technique feedback is difficult to quantify consistently

Coaches often see movement issues, but objective comparison across sessions and athletes remains limited.

2
Existing systems are often too expensive or too rigid

Venue-fixed camera setups and premium performance platforms can be out of reach for growing academies and university programs.

3
Many wearables do not offer joint-level technique insight

Physiological or load-focused trackers may help with exertion data, but not with movement quality itself.

4
Institutions need practical tools, not research-only systems

Academies need solutions that fit real training routines, team workflows, and budget constraints.

Solution

KineticSense is a modular sports motion analysis platform built for team environments

Instead of locking analysis into one venue, one body position, or one sport, KineticSense combines a detachable IMU sensor, sport-specific accessories, mobile real-time feedback, and a coach dashboard in one institution-friendly platform.

01

Detachable 9-DOF sensor

A compact motion sensing module captures training data and moves between accessories instead of requiring separate dedicated hardware for every use case.

  • Single sensor concept
  • Detachable and reusable across accessories
  • Designed for practical deployment
02

Multiple textile accessories

Arm, wrist, shoulder, knee, ankle, and shirt-based options allow different movement patterns to be captured depending on sport and coaching need.

  • Flexible body placement
  • Sport-specific usage options
  • Supports broader team workflows
03

Mobile real-time feedback

During training, athletes and staff can view technique-oriented session feedback without waiting for a full post-session analysis cycle.

  • Live session visibility
  • Technique score output
  • Faster training adjustments
04

Coach dashboard

After the session, coaches can review athlete reports, follow trend development, compare sessions, and add performance notes inside a team-ready interface.

  • Team-level reporting
  • Trend visualization
  • Session review workflow
05

Sport-adaptive AI

KineticSense is designed to route movement analysis logic based on sport context and accessory position, reducing setup friction for coaches.

  • Context-aware analysis logic
  • More relevant technique scoring
  • Simpler coach experience
06

Coach annotation loop

Coaches can label repetitions and add notes, creating a feedback loop that improves future analysis quality over time.

  • Human-in-the-loop improvement
  • Institutional learning value
  • Supports long-term product accuracy
How It Works

From wearable setup to coach-ready reporting in six steps

KineticSense is designed to fit how organizations already train: quick setup, structured session capture, and clear post-session review.

1

Attach the sensor

Choose the appropriate textile accessory for the athlete and drill, then attach the detachable motion module.

2

Start the session

Open the mobile app, begin the training session, and connect the athlete setup with minimal friction.

3

Capture movement data

The system streams motion data during training and prepares it for technique-oriented analysis.

4

Receive technique scoring

Real-time session feedback helps staff review form quality and consistency while the athlete is still training.

5

Review athlete reports

Coaches access session summaries, trend views, and performance indicators inside the web dashboard.

6

Improve future analysis

Coach notes and repetition annotations support a stronger feedback loop for future scoring and reporting.
